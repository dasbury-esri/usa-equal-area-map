## Step 3: Canonical Query Grids

Tolerance levels are anchored at scale 1000000 with fixed factors of square
root of two. The level is `floor(2 * log2(scale / 1000000))`, with a numerical
boundary guard. Rounding is toward finer detail, never deliberately coarser than
the current half-pixel generalization. At scale 24000000 the request tolerance
is **0.026890214759459675 degrees**. Refresh decisions now compare these same
levels, rather than a relative threshold tied to whichever scale last loaded.

Geographic bounds, including existing pane margins and the two-degree guard,
round outward to multiples of 5 degrees before dateline splitting and merging.
Boxes are sorted before and after merging so pane ordering does not change
serialization. Unit checks cover slight center jitter, reversed pane order,
dateline splits and every supported tolerance boundary. Identical cold/warm
geometry URLs are independently compared in the native HTTP benchmark.

**No ArcGIS Online shared-cache hit is demonstrated by these repeats.**
Stable URLs enable browser reuse here, but repeats in a new profile still
transfer the geometry and have similar startup times. RFC 9111
[section 5.2.2.7](https://httpwg.org/specs/rfc9111.html#cache-response-directive.private)
forbids shared HTTP caches from storing an unqualified `private` response,
while permitting private browser caches. This does not establish whether an
unexposed internal service/database cache was used. No undocumented meaning
of the vendor header or backend-cache conclusion is assumed from timing alone.
